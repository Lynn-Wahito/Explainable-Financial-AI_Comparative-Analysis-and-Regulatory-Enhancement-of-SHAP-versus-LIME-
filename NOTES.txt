MODEL INFORMATION
	•	Model used: XGBoost (predicts credit risk)
	•	Dataset used: UCI Credit Card Default Dataset
	•	Database: SQLite (via SQLAlchemy ORM)
	•	Goal: Predict the chance of loan default and explain the results using SHAP and LIME

⸻

SEX
1 → Male
2 → Female

⸻

EDUCATION
1 → Graduate School
2 → University
3 → High School
4 → Others or Unknown

(Some datasets include 0, 5, or 6 but these are treated as “unknown.”)

⸻

MARRIAGE
1 → Married
2 → Single
3 → Others (Divorced or Unknown)

⸻

PAYMENT HISTORY (PAY_0 to PAY_6)
These show the customer’s repayment behavior for the last six months.
PAY_0 represents the most recent month, while PAY_6 is six months ago.

Values:
-1 → Paid on time
0 → Paid fully
1 → 1 month late
2 → 2 months late
3 to 9 → 3 to 9 months late

Higher numbers mean more late payments (riskier).
Lower or negative values mean timely payments (less risky).

⸻

FINANCIAL FIELDS
LIMIT_BAL → Credit limit approved
BILL_AMT1–6 → Total bill for each month
PAY_AMT1–6 → Amount actually paid each month
